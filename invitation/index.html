What each button would do in the real invitation
  • Show me — opens a ready email to Bill saying so. Bill follows up with the first look: a short piece of Bill and Marlowe actually working together, to watch.
  • Tell me more — opens a ready email to Bill with the question. Bill writes back with a calm explanation, in plain words, of what they’d be witnessing and how often anything would arrive.
  • Not yet — opens a ready email to Bill saving their seat. Nothing more is sent until they ask — the kind answer gets the same respect as the eager one.
